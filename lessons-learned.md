# Lessons Learned

## Purpose

This is a living document for durable coaching knowledge — patterns confirmed over time, not observations from a single day. It exists so that insight earned through actual coaching experience persists across sessions instead of being re-derived or forgotten.

---

## What Belongs Here

- Long-term responses to training (how the athlete tends to adapt to a given type of stimulus).
- Successful interventions (a specific adjustment that reliably helped).
- Persistent recovery patterns (recurring signals that precede good or poor outcomes).
- Athlete preferences that meaningfully influence coaching.
- Lessons supported by repeated evidence, not a single instance.

---

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Temporary observations or one-off events.
- Daily logs or workout summaries — those belong in `logs/` or `reviews/`.
- Anything not yet supported by repeated evidence. A single data point is an observation, not a lesson.

---

## Entry Format

Each entry should be dated and state the pattern, the evidence behind it, and its coaching implication:

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

Pattern: What was observed, stated plainly.
Evidence: What supports this being a durable pattern rather than a coincidence (timeframe, number of instances).
Implication: How this should influence future coaching decisions.
```

This section currently contains no real entries — only the template above. Entries should be added here as durable patterns are confirmed through actual coaching over time.

---

## Review Process

This document should be reviewed periodically alongside `goals.md`. Entries that no longer hold (e.g., a pattern that was specific to a since-resolved circumstance) should be revised or removed rather than left to accumulate as stale guidance.
